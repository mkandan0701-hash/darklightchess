# Coimbatore Backlink Strategy — Darklight Chess Academy

Ethical, relationship-based backlink opportunities only. Nothing here involves buying links, PBNs, link farms, comment spam, or automated mass outreach — all of it requires an actual human relationship or a genuine piece of newsworthy/useful content.

## Where the links could realistically come from

### Schools & school chess programs
Coimbatore schools that run (or would like to run) a chess club or inter-house chess tournament are a natural fit — offer to help judge, coach, or supply a coach for a one-off workshop in exchange for nothing but a mention/link on the school's website or newsletter if they publish one.

### Colleges
Local colleges with a chess club or annual cultural fest sometimes look for outside coaches or tournament arbiters for their chess events — this is a genuine, mutually useful connection, and college event pages often link to participating organizations.

### Tournament organizers
Any organizer running an open or rated tournament in or near Coimbatore is a potential partner — sponsoring a small prize, helping with arbiting, or simply having Darklight listed as a "supporting academy" on the tournament's own webpage is a legitimate, contextually relevant link.

### Chess associations
The Tamil Nadu State Chess Association and district-level chess bodies sometimes publish academy/coach directories or news of local events — only pursue this if there's a genuine, verifiable affiliation or event connection (never claim one that doesn't exist).

### Local newspapers & local blogs
Coimbatore editions of regional newspapers, and independent local blogs covering city life/education, occasionally cover local sports and education stories. A genuine student achievement, a notable tournament the academy hosts, or a workshop for underprivileged children are the kind of stories that get covered on merit — not press releases about the business itself.

### Parenting websites & kids-activity websites
Sites that list activities/classes for children in Coimbatore (or India more broadly) are a natural, relevant place for a listing — this overlaps with `docs/local-seo-citations.md`, but a genuinely useful guest contribution (e.g. "how to pick an extracurricular activity for your child") can also earn an editorial link, not just a directory listing.

### Event websites
If the academy participates in or sponsors a local kids'/education fair, expo, or community event, event websites frequently link to participating organizations.

### Community organizations
Residents' welfare associations (especially in Saibaba Colony and nearby areas), community centres, and cultural associations sometimes host activity days or host visiting instructors — a genuine community workshop can lead to a natural mention on the organization's own site or social channels.

### Sports facilities
Local sports clubs or multi-activity centres in Coimbatore that don't offer chess themselves are potential referral partners — a shared-referral relationship (they send chess enquiries to Darklight, and vice versa for other sports) is a natural, human relationship that can also produce a website link.

## Outreach ideas (all require doing something genuinely useful first — the link is a byproduct, not the ask)

1. **Tournament coverage** — after running or participating in a notable tournament, write up a short, honest recap (results, a couple of photos, nothing embellished) and offer it to a local blog or the tournament organizer's own site as a guest recap.
2. **Chess workshops** — offer a free one-off chess workshop to a school, community centre, or residents' association. This is a genuine community contribution first; if they publicize the event on their own site, that's a natural link.
3. **School collaborations** — propose an ongoing after-school chess club partnership with a nearby school. A long-term relationship like this often earns a standing link on the school's "activities" or "partners" page.
4. **Guest educational articles** — pitch a genuinely useful, non-promotional article (e.g. "how chess supports a child's focus and patience") to a parenting website or local blog that accepts guest contributions. The value has to stand on its own; a link back to Darklight in the author bio is the reasonable ask.
5. **Student achievement stories** — when a student does something genuinely noteworthy at a real tournament, a short, factual write-up (only real, verifiable results — never invented ones) is the kind of story local papers/blogs sometimes pick up.
6. **Local event partnerships** — look for existing Coimbatore kids'/education events looking for an activity partner or sponsor rather than always initiating a brand-new event.

## What this document deliberately does not recommend

- Buying links or "guest post packages" from SEO resellers.
- Private blog networks (PBNs) or link farms.
- Automated or mass outreach email campaigns.
- Comment spam on unrelated blogs/forums.
- Directory submissions purely for the link, with no real business presence behind them.

## How to track this

Keep a simple running list (a spreadsheet is fine) of: who was contacted, what was offered, what came of it, and whether a link was actually placed. This is slow, relationship-based work — expect it to produce a handful of genuinely strong local links over months, not dozens of low-quality ones overnight.
