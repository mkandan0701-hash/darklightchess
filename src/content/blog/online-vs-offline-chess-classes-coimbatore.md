Darklight teaches both formats every week — [online batches](/online-chess-classes/) across 30+ countries and offline centers in [Coimbatore](/chess-academy-coimbatore/). Neither is "better"; they suit different families. Here's the honest comparison we give every parent at counselling.

## Choose online if…

You live outside Coimbatore or outside India; your evenings are tight and travel time matters; your child is comfortable on a screen and enjoys digital tools. Our online sessions use live demonstration boards, instant puzzle sharing and game reviews — one of our online-only students recently won a tournament in Canada.

## Choose offline if…

You are in or near Coimbatore; your child focuses better away from screens; you value the ritual of sitting at a real board with a coach across the table. Physical classes also make it easier for younger children (4–7) to stay engaged for a full session.

## The honest truth

Progress depends far more on consistency than on format. A student who attends two online classes a week reliably will outpace one who attends offline classes irregularly. Pick the format your family can sustain.

## Or mix both

Several of our Coimbatore families do offline weekend classes plus a midweek online session. Hybrid schedules are absolutely possible — just ask.
